Hello, I'm Sicheng.

I’m interested in how machines understand the physical world — and learn to act in it.

At Peking University, I work on world models and embodied intelligence. I’m drawn to the gap between a model that generates something convincing and one that faithfully responds to an action.

With WorldSimProbe, I study that gap through simulator evaluation. With CIFT, I explore how the composition of real and generated data shapes robot generalization.

I also enjoy the engineering that makes research possible: working with data, training models, and getting inference and evaluation to run well.

A few papers, a few experiments, and plenty of open questions.
